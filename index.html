    FOLD SPACE AND TIME

    T cycles between five target eras. Q rotates a 24m facet and stamps it with that era. Native-era troops gain an advantage; analog units can defeat future prediction. Z rewinds your position and health five seconds.

    BUILD AN ARMY

    TAB opens live commander mode. In war rifts, recruit battalions, build production facilities, break twin shield anchors and destroy the enemy citadel. Optional chronal crises reward research. Enemy commanders adapt their battalion choices; control mixed-era units in the same army.

    SURVIVE THE FRONTIER

    E interact/harvest/enter Rift Gate. H medkit · J ration · K water · M enlarge map. Return to home camp to rest. Use camps, consumables and your buildings to stay alive.

    LIVE IN FIVE WORLDS

    Medieval is your starting era. Four ADVANCED RIFTS near your home lead to other infinite procedural frontier worlds—ancient, industrial, network-age and far future. Each has unique landmarks and a war campaign. War rifts are 36-minute RTS conflicts; classic operation rifts retain the six-stage shooter campaign.

    LOST FUTURES

    Press Y to open the Doctrine Weave. Choose any commander heritage regardless of current world. Research alternate sciences from all eras, then equip up to three together. Stone harmonics, living computers, mechanical oracles and gravity gardens are equally sophisticated paths. Nothing was nuked. Public time travel made jurisdiction, trade and power collapse as entire empires accessed other centuries. Campaign victories and survey discoveries yield CHRONICLE INSIGHT for ancestral, engineering and oracle upgrades. Worlds have no single required order. Multiplayer and city-scale AAA content remain future work.
