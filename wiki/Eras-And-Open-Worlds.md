# Eras & Open Worlds

## The difference between Advanced Rifts and War Rifts

**Advanced Rifts** take you to a separate open-world environment in another historical period. They can be used in any order, and returning restores your last location in the visited era. Each world is seeded differently, with its own palette, sky, NPC witnesses, landmarks, campaign gate, lore, and opportunities for Chronicle Insight. Personal equipment, quest records and recruitable companions follow you.

**War Rifts** take you to authored-concept battle scenarios inside the selected era. The current prototype offers five chronal war scenarios plus the three older multi-stage PvE shooter operations. The war mode tracks its own supply economy and battlefield army; when it ends, you return to your original overworld era and receive persistent RPG rewards.

### Exploration and longevity

In v0.4, each era has **three unique explorable survey sites**. A first-time survey yields Chronicle Insight and resources. Explored sites unlock caravan jumps for Flux. Each era has original named NPCs (in addition to the medieval campaign's existing cast), including merchants and recruitable companions. Outdoor terrain and props are seeded and streamed around the player; **these are not yet Skyrim-size authored quest maps**.

Production concept: one huge living world per era with settlements, handcrafted dungeon chains, wildlife, markets, taverns, faction patrols, merchant schedules, city laws, random encounters and long quest arcs. A world atlas needs population-density targets and reusable modular environment kits rather than simply vast empty terrain. The entire chronology should be unlocked non-linearly—no obligatory prehistoric → industrial → future ladder.

## Encounters across chronology

- A medieval village hires an 1889 telegraph union to secretly organize a peasant revolt.
- A 2086 corporation imports pack animals that cannot be tracked by its own drone-based logistics.
- An ancient hunter sells a route across an ambush field to an Oracle Consortium general, then leads another expedition through an unrecorded animal tunnel.
- A monastery becomes a neutral archive because a single librarian has collected both the first and last editions of the same book.
- A future station possesses a hand-forged lock that no AI can open because it was built with random physical imperfections.

## Scope flags

**Working:** five distinct procedural maps, non-linear travel portals, position memory per world, local NPCs, unique survey rewards, campaign war portals, chrono-logs, persistent build/gear/save systems.

**Planned:** large handcrafted cities, actual medieval combat animation and visual fidelity, hundreds of authored quest arcs, streamed continental-scale terrain, vehicles and mounts, regional diplomacy, travel weather and seasons, living economy, NPC daily schedules and thousands of persistent citizens.
