# RIFTFRONT v0.3 — The Living Frontier RPG update

## Why this update exists

CROWNFOLD had a useful shooter-and-squad core, but the world between its operations lacked character-building choices, authored discoveries and reasons to return to settlements. This update supplies a compact systems-driven RPG loop without pretending the game has already become a multimillion-dollar authored open world. The goal is **interactions between systems** rather than a large unchecked catalog.

## Five satisfying loops

**Hunt → loot → forge → specialize.** Find enemies or world caches, receive randomized gear in armor/relic/mod slots, compare stat bonuses, craft upgraded equipment, salvage unneeded items and spend earned perks in warfare, survival, mastery, arcana or command. The rarer items have stronger numerical bonuses; no cosmetic-only rarity tiers.

**Explore → meet people → open vaults.** Talk to named NPCs with personal dialogue and unique quest roles, accept an objective, discover a landmark, enter one of three unique 3D dungeon locations, clear the enemy guards around its two relays, defeat the guardian, claim a relic and return to the giver. Unlock fast travel as you explore.

**Build → recruit → defend → expand.** Your turrets, barricades, recovery shelters, bastions, greenhouses, refineries and Flux beacons work in real time. Recruit companions and ordinary soldiers, order them around in the live commander view and empower them with War Chant or the Commander perk tree. Permanent overworld buildings remain between saves.

**Take risks → solve a lock → escape or get caught.** Sneak near an NPC and choose the one-time pickpocket option. A failed attempt builds a real fine and damages standing with the NPC's faction; seek out Marshal Rhys to pay it. Open the three newly placed mechanical safes using a frequency slider and proximity hints; unused picks are resources you can craft.

**Choose your world → keep playing.** The main investigation advances across three lost dungeons. After completing the arc, the Pale Archive leader can let you shape the future of the Warden, Freefolk or Archive systems. In this build, that gives lightweight persistent reputation changes and a mechanical world modifier—not a fully scripted ending cinematic. Complete repeatable bounties to continue progression after quests are done.

## What is *not* present

The characters currently have dialogue choices, not cinematic conversations with branching voiced performance. The quests are compact objective-driven missions, not a huge tree of actor-scheduled side stories. NPCs do not follow a full day/night schedule or autonomously lead families and businesses. The safe puzzle is a frequency-guessing dial rather than a detailed physics lock; theft is a one-time risk check, not simulation of inventory per person. Dungeons have three differentiated themes and enemy sets but a shared two-relay/gatekeeper structure. World consequences are currently gameplay modifiers rather than entire transformed towns. No mount system, vehicles, huge handcrafted city interiors, cooking minigame, companion romance, character creator, large-scale destructible physics, official Bethesda content or network co-op is claimed.

## Quality next steps for a real AA/AAA development effort

Prioritize third-person aiming, character rigs and animation; hand-designed encounter flow; line-of-sight/cover/navmesh navigation; real companion personalities and NPC world schedules; scalable world streaming; believable economy; additional dungeon mechanics; richer faction consequences; more adversary archetypes and raid directors; authored voice/text without grind; true accessible settings and robust saves; eventual co-op only after single-player feel is excellent. Evaluate the native Unreal architecture in `docs/TECH_STACK_2026.md` on physical hardware. **Do not migrate to an engine merely for a fashionable technology name without performance and production justification.**
